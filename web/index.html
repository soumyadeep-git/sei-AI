    How a call works

    One pipeline, one model pass per turn.

    1. 01

      The caller speaks

      Twilio streams the call audio. Silero detects the turn, Deepgram Nova transcribes it.

    2. 02

      The model decides

      Groq's gpt-oss-120b answers from the six articles in its prompt, or calls a tool when they don't cover the question.

    3. 03

      The agent replies

      Deepgram Aura-2 speaks one to three short sentences. The caller can talk over it.

    4. 04

      The call ends cleanly

      A handoff, a goodbye or a long silence plays a fixed closing line in full, then hangs up.

    Answers

    The six articles

    1. Checking the status of a transfer
    2. When the money will arrive
    3. Why it says complete before the money arrives
    4. Why it's taking longer than the estimate
    5. What a proof of payment is
    6. What a banking partner reference number is

    Hands to a human

    Everything else

    1. Fees, exchange rates, cards, accounts and sign-up
    2. Anything that needs the caller's own account
    3. Questions unrelated to Wise
    4. Requests to ignore the rules or change role

    Design decisions

    Small on purpose.

    01

    Knowledge in the prompt, not RAG

    The articles are about 2k tokens. Retrieval would add latency and a way to miss the right chunk.

    02

    Deflection is a tool call

    The same model pass that answers also decides scope, with the whole conversation in view. No extra classifier round trip.

    03

    Fixed closing lines

    Endings are scripted and can't be interrupted, so the agent never promises what a human then has to honour.

    04

    Only Twilio can start a call

    Media streams must carry Twilio's signature. The browser demo is capped and serves one caller at a time.
